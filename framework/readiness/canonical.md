# Canonical Software-System Lifecycle Bootstrap Framework and Kit Readiness Criterion

The framework and starter kit are ready when they are:

- normalized;
- irreducible;
- materially complete;
- internally coherent;
- operationally realizable;
- assurable;

across their intended software-system and lifecycle domain.

They must support governed, bidirectionally traceable progression from an initial idea through Subject, Telos, Purpose, Outcomes, Capabilities, Functions, Mechanisms, Requirements, implementation, executable runtime behavior, operation, evolution, and retirement without requiring foundational redesign.

Normative authority, human governance, AI agency, automation, contextual applicability, and change over time are required material scope conditions rather than additional peer readiness dimensions.

A material failure in any of the six readiness dimensions means canonical readiness is not established.
