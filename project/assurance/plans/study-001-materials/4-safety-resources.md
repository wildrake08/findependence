# Help, if you ever need it

*Everyone taking part gets this sheet. It doesn't mean anything about you.*

These services are free and confidential, and you don't have to be in a crisis to call. You can ask about money being used to control you, as well as other kinds of harm.

| Who | How to reach them |
|---|---|
| **Emergency** | Call **911** |
| **National Domestic Violence Hotline**, 24/7 | Call **1-800-799-7233** · Text **START** to **88788** · TTY **1-800-787-3224** |
| **988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline**, 24/7 | Call or text **988** |
| **Washington State domestic violence line** | [NUMBER — VERIFY BEFORE USE] |
| **Local support in Pierce County** | [ORGANIZATION AND NUMBER — VERIFY BEFORE USE] |
| **Help with money problems** (free or low-cost, non-profit) | [LOCAL ORGANIZATION — VERIFY BEFORE USE] |

**If someone checks your phone or papers:** you can keep this sheet somewhere else, or just remember **988** and **911**.

*The researcher is not a counselor and can't give advice, but can help you find the right service.*
